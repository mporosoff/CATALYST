## Install CATALYST

CATALYST is a single file. There is nothing else to install.

| Your computer | Download (from **Assets** below) |
| --- | --- |
| Windows 10/11 | `CATALYST-Windows.exe` |
| Mac with Apple silicon (M1–M4) | `CATALYST-Mac-AppleSilicon.dmg` |
| Mac with Intel | `CATALYST-Mac-Intel.dmg` |

Not sure which Mac you have? Apple menu → **About This Mac** → "Chip" (Apple) or "Processor" (Intel).

### Windows

1. Download `CATALYST-Windows.exe`. Move it somewhere you'll find it again, such as your Desktop or Documents.
2. Double-click it.
3. The first time, Windows may say *"Windows protected your PC"*. Click **More info** → **Run anyway**. This happens because the app isn't signed by a commercial publisher.

### Mac

1. Download the `.dmg` for your Mac, open it, and drag **CATALYST** into **Applications**.
2. The first time, **right-click** (or Control-click) CATALYST in Applications → **Open** → **Open**.
3. If macOS still refuses: **System Settings → Privacy & Security** → scroll down → **Open Anyway**.

### First start

Enter your name, initials and lab. Then paste your lab's SciSure token (ask the CATALYST coordinator) and click **Connect**. That's it.

### Updates

When a new version is out, CATALYST shows an **Update** button in its sidebar. Click it, download the new file, and replace the old one. Your profile, drafts and saved token carry over.

A step-by-step guide is available in the app under **Help**.
